European Dream maintains a differentiated presence across online platforms in order to balance reach, coherence, editorial quality, and alignment with our values.

Not all platforms play the same role, and not all are actively used at this stage.

## Editorial Principle

European Dream does not aim to maximise presence across all platforms indiscriminately.

Our approach prioritises consistency, clarity, and meaningful interaction over visibility alone. Platform usage may evolve over time as the project develops.

---

## Overview

### Actively Used Platforms

These platforms are our primary channels for communication, collaboration, and publication. They are actively maintained and reflect our current editorial tone.

- **[Mastodon](https://mastodon.social/@european_dream)** — Announcements, open discussions, and decentralised dialogue.
- **[Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/european-dream.eu)** — Announcements, short-form updates and interactions.
- **[GitHub](https://github.com/European-Dream/)** — Documentation, collaborative work, and source code publication.
- **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/european-dream/)** — Announcements, institutional articles, and outreach.
- **[Medium](https://medium.com/@european_dream)** — Long-form articles and essays exploring the themes and questions at the core of the European Dream initiative.

### Relay-Only Platforms

These platforms are used in a limited manner, primarily to relay links to new website pages or publications, **without** platform-specific engagement.

- **[X (ex-Twitter)](https://x.com/EuropeanDream_)** — Link sharing only.

## Strategy

### Website

#### Website Role

Our authoritative home. The website hosts official publications — papers and analyses that have been deliberated and accepted through community consensus — as well as foundational documents, project structure, and onboarding materials. It is the canonical source for what European Dream is and stands for.

#### Website Tone

Clear, welcoming, and civic-minded. Official without being cold. The website speaks for the initiative as a whole, not for individual voices.

#### Website Profile

**HTML Meta Description**

```text
European Dream is a non-profit civic initiative, rooted in the European Union, exploring and experimenting with post-transactional forms of social organisation.
```

### Mastodon

#### Mastodon Role

Our primary space for decentralised dialogue and community building. Mastodon's values — openness, federation, and resistance to centralised control — align closely with our vision. We use it for announcements, open discussions, and exchanges with like-minded communities in the Fediverse.

#### Mastodon Tone

Warm, community-oriented, and values-driven. More conversational than LinkedIn, with an emphasis on connection and shared purpose. We speak as fellow travellers, not as an institution addressing an audience.

#### Mastodon Profile

**Bio**

```text
Dreaming and building a post-transactional world, rooted in the European Union.

A non-profit civic initiative for those ready to question, debate, and experiment with what comes after transactional societies. Join the conversation.

#Humanity #Sociology #Economics #EuropeanUnion #EU
```

### Bluesky

#### Bluesky Role

A space for discovery, short-form updates, and casual interaction. Bluesky's growing community includes many users interested in social innovation, technology ethics, and alternative futures. We use it to share updates, engage in lighter exchanges, and reach audiences who may not yet know us.

#### Bluesky Tone

Accessible and conversational. Slightly more casual than other platforms, while remaining thoughtful. We can be playful here, though always grounded in our values.

#### Bluesky Profile

**Description**

```text
Dreaming and building a post-transactional world, rooted in the European Union.

https://european-dream.eu
```

### GitHub

#### GitHub Role

Our technical home for documentation, collaborative work, and open-source publication. GitHub serves contributors who want to participate in building the project's infrastructure, improving documentation, or exploring our materials. It signals our commitment to transparency and open collaboration.

#### GitHub Tone

Clear, functional, and contributor-friendly. We write for people who want to understand what the project does and how they can help. Warmth is welcome, but clarity comes first.

#### GitHub Profile

**Description**

```text
Dreaming and building a post-transactional world, rooted in the European Union.
```

### LinkedIn

#### LinkedIn Role

Our institutional face. LinkedIn reaches professionals, researchers, and organisations who may be interested in our work or in collaboration. We use it for announcements, longer-form articles, and outreach to potential partners, supporters, or curious professionals.

#### LinkedIn Tone

Professional and institutional, but inviting. We present ourselves as a credible civic initiative while keeping the door open to participation. Less casual than Mastodon or Bluesky, but never cold or corporate.

#### LinkedIn Profile

**Tagline**

```text
Dreaming and building a post-transactional world, rooted in the European Union.
```

**Overview**

```text
We believe humanity can move beyond societies where nearly all relationships – with one another and with our environment – are mediated through transactions.

European Dream is a non-profit civic initiative, rooted in the European Union, that brings together people ready to question these foundations and experiment with alternatives. We research, debate, and design new forms of social organisation – grounded in small-scale, real-life experiments.

We call this vision Holomutualism: a world where relationships are built on helpfulness rather than exchange, where freedom grows through decentralisation, and where humanity can prosper in happiness, wisdom, and knowledge.

Whether you are a researcher, a practitioner, or simply someone who resonates with these questions – you are welcome to join the conversation.
```

### Medium

#### Medium Role

Our space for long-form reflection, interviews, essays, and the European Dream Weekly Journal. Medium reaches readers who enjoy thoughtful, narrative content.

We publish here content that has not been tested against community-wide consensus: personal essays, open questions, hypotheses, and individual or small-group perspectives. Interviews — with everyday EU citizens and recognised voices alike — also appear here.

By contrast, finalised papers and analyses that have gone through our collective deliberation process are published as official publications on the main website. Medium is where we think; the website is where we conclude.

#### Medium Tone

Reflective, narrative, and personal — especially for the Weekly Journal. Essays may be more analytical, but always human. This is where we think aloud, test ideas, and invite readers into our process rather than presenting polished conclusions.
